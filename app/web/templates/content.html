ATAK data packages

A data package is a zip carrying MANIFEST/manifest.xml. ATAK imports it and files the contents itself, so nothing here needs a destination. ⚠️ Delivered to a device once and never re-sent — ATAK consumes the file, and importing the same package repeatedly can take the app down.
